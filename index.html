Full data description · Original CSV

A consistent price rule

All views use sale prices from $10,000 through $5,000,000, inclusive. Original records are unchanged.

Time to find a buyer

Days on market = contract date − list date. Missing dates stay missing; only nonnegative intervals enter the median. Coverage is shown beside the metric. Missing dates are not random, so compare cities with care.

Each sale stays a sale

Listing ID identifies each record. Repeated street addresses are kept: separate units and repeat sales can share an address. Campus areas use the dataset’s nearest-campus assignment.

Know what is left out

Price per sq ft uses positive, reported living area. Home type is unreliable and is not used for filtering. Lot size, bathroom counts, and taxes contain anomalies and are not used in these metrics. City medians reflect different mixes of homes; distance does not establish causation.
